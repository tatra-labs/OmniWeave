## Context

Five defect rows and two adjacent ones, all of the same shape: **the charter prints a name — a format, a media type, a pass, a card key, a term in a rule — and nothing in the plan produces or defines it.** Three answers are available for each: give it a producer, give it a definition, or record its absence with a gate that fails when the record and the roster diverge. This ADR gives each row one of the three. `V01-17` is the mechanical closer for the third answer and is the reason the group holds together: "Every format class in §2.1 either has an enabled Driver whose card's `formats` list covers it, or is absent from the shipped scope with a named reason — asserted mechanically against `[drivers] enabled`" ([00](../00-vision.md):718).

**Why a decision and not a repair.** Four of the seven rows are already answered *somewhere* — `06` §5.1 defines `merge_hops`, `06` §1.8 contains `phase`, `05` §4.4 routes twelve tokens, `00` §2.1 records HTML and audio as out. What no document can do is rule that its answer is **the** answer while the charter prints a different one, because the charter is immutable and every one of these is a charter locus. That is the whole content of this ADR: six errata and one ratification.

**One finding about the partition, stated because it is more useful than pretending coherence.** Five of the seven rows are about what is in the shipped surface. **`06#T2` is not.** `merge_hops` is a tie-break inside the entity lane's union-find; it changes no scope statement and no reader's belief about what ships. It sits in ADR-8 because it is `06`'s *other* charter tension — grouped by document of origin, not by question. It is decided here rather than moved, because moving one row across an ADR partition costs more than the row does; but a future reader should not infer from its presence that the survivor rule is a scope question.

## Decision

**D8.1 — the counts.** anydoc's `Block` enum is **eight** variants, verified at `_collections/anydoc/src/model/block.rs:5-38`. Every plan document printing "nine variants" is corrected: [01](../01-principles.md):291, [03](../03-document-model.md):1501, [05](../05-ingest-and-routing.md):3139, [06](../06-structure-extraction.md):2053, [07](../07-store-and-retrieval.md):2438, [18](../18-api-sketch.md):1184. The docling citation is `standard_pdf_pipeline.py:867-871`, over a field defaulted at `base_models.py:419`; [03](../03-document-model.md) §Q10's ":867-870" is corrected in the same pass. **The shipped pass ladder is fourteen passes**, verified by counting `pass_id` rows in both printed tables; [04](../04-driver-system.md):1357, [04](../04-driver-system.md):2613 and [17](../17-risks.md):262 are corrected.

**D8.2 — `parse.office.anydoc`'s `[capability] formats` is twelve media types, one per token `decode.office-native` routes**, and [05](../05-ingest-and-routing.md) §4.4 is the sole home of the token list:

```toml
[capability]
formats = ["application/msword",                                                   # doc
           "application/vnd.openxmlformats-officedocument.wordprocessingml.document",  # docx
           "application/vnd.ms-powerpoint",                                        # ppt
           "application/vnd.openxmlformats-officedocument.presentationml.presentation", # pptx
           "application/vnd.ms-excel",                                             # xls
           "application/vnd.openxmlformats-officedocument.spreadsheetml.sheet",     # xlsx
           "application/vnd.oasis.opendocument.text",                              # odt
           "application/vnd.oasis.opendocument.spreadsheet",                       # ods
           "application/vnd.oasis.opendocument.presentation",                      # odp
           "application/rtf", "application/epub+zip", "text/csv"]                  # rtf epub csv
```

`pdf` is **excluded** although anydoc serves it: `parse.pdf.pdfium` owns the PDF path and `decode.office-native`'s own comment says "minus pdf". The invariant is stated once and enforced by check 11: **the card's `formats` and the rule's tokens are images of each other under `format_token_for`**, so neither can drift alone. [00](../00-vision.md) §2.1's parenthetical "(anydoc's fourteen formats)" becomes "the twelve office media types `decode.office-native` routes" — **fourteen is anydoc's own benchmark denominator, not a capability count**: `_collections/anydoc/README.md:174` reports "fourteen formats" and the per-format table it points at (`:190-202`) has **thirteen** rows. Nothing in omniweave may cite it as a served-format count again. [12](../12-performance.md):220 and :1893 and [04](../04-driver-system.md):1575 keep "fourteen formats" **only** where they are quoting the benchmark, and must say so.

**D8.3 — `derive.*.vlm` is out of the release-1 ladder.** [charter](../_notes/charter.md):345's and :8230's `omniweave-vision` row reads `parse.page.olmocr` only. The charter's own rule is applied in the direction it was not written for: :5477-5480 says "a pass in the shipped ladder whose distribution does not exist is a promise nothing keeps", and a *distribution* naming passes that are in no ladder, no `enable` list and no `[drivers] enabled` is the same promise from the other side. A VLM figure and chart pass, if it ever ships, is named the way `derive.code.treesitter` is ([06](../06-structure-extraction.md):935-939) — **out of tree, named so nobody expects it in the default install**. It needs a `pass_id`, a rank and phase, a lane, a distribution row and an `enable` entry, and gets them in the ADR that adds it, not before.

**D8.4 — `phase` moves to the `[derive]` sibling table.** [04](../04-driver-system.md):629-634's `[derive]` block gains a fifth key, `phase = 50`, and `[capability.derive]` ([06](../06-structure-extraction.md):618-621) loses it. This is not an override of the charter but an application of it: [charter](../_notes/charter.md):1716 already rules that "non-monotone declarations live in a sibling table instead", `[derive]` is already that table and is already marked "NON-MONOTONE. Unknown key = HARD ERROR", and only the parenthetical at :4893 says otherwise. The ordering key `(cost_rank, phase)` and `cost_rank`'s derivation from `cost_class` are untouched, so "deterministic before LLM" remains a property of the ledger. **The safety of the move is the reason to make it now**: a dropped `phase` under `[capability.derive]` is *ignored* with `CARD_CAPABILITY_UNKNOWN`; under `[derive]` an unknown key is a hard error, which is the correct disposition for a key whose default (50) sits in the middle of its range rather than at its weakest end.

**D8.5 — `merge_hops` stands as [06](../06-structure-extraction.md) §5.1 defines it, with `06#N4` as the pre-committed trigger.** The survivor of a union is `min` on `(merge_hops, -len(alias_set), key)`; `merge_hops(e)` is the depth of `e` in the union-find forest built by processing that component's live positive pairs in the total order `(-COALESCE(score, 100.0), min(key), max(key))`, **before path compression**, zero for a node that was a set representative throughout. The rebuild-from-log rule is part of the definition and not an optimisation note: the closure is rebuilt from the whole live positive pair set of the affected component on every recomputation and is **never** updated in place. **The fallback is pre-committed and named:** `06#N4`'s measurement — build one corpus fully and once incrementally, diff the `(key, canonical.key)` pair multiset — is the falsifier. If that multiset ever differs while `canonical_projection()` matches, the rule switches to `min on (-len(alias_set), -doc_count, key)` and the rebuild rule is deleted with it. Until it runs, this is the decision and not a deferral.

**D8.6 — HTML, email, audio and video stay out, and the charter's §1 list is not amended.** [00](../00-vision.md) §2.1:118-119 is the shipped input surface and charter §1's opening sentence is a statement of purpose, not of roster. `V01-17`'s own second branch — "or is absent from the shipped scope with a named reason" — is written for exactly this and passes on both rows as they stand. Audio and video additionally carry a **model** precondition, recorded so the v2 question is well posed: `Locus.precision` has no temporal member and `OriginSpan` has no time branch, so time-based media is a `SCHEMA` change under [charter](../_notes/charter.md) §6.6's two-release window, not a Driver. Neither row costs an erratum.

None of D1-D10 is re-issued: D7's ladder, D2's `origin_span` ceiling and D3's card grammar all keep their conclusions — every row below corrects a count, a citation or a placement, and none changes a decision.

## Alternatives rejected

### Read `05` §4.4's twelve tokens as the correction and leave the card alone

The minimal reading, and the one `00#V-Q9`'s closer might be taken to license: `05` §4.4 already routes twelve tokens, `00` §2.1 already says "fourteen formats", and a reader who wants the shipped set has it. Rejected because the card is not documentation — `[capability] formats` is the **input to `format_token_for`** and therefore to check 11 and to `V01-17`. A five-entry list is not an abbreviation; it is a declaration that seven of the tokens the shipped policy routes are formats the driver refuses, and `resolve()` filters on `format in capability.formats` ([charter](../_notes/charter.md):2173-2174) before anything else. Left alone, `ow parse deck.pptx` returns `FORMAT_UNSUPPORTED` on the shipped install.

### Widen the card to anydoc's full twenty-one extensions

Genuinely the more generous reading of "no plan document may read that five-entry list as the shipped format set", and it is what anydoc actually supports: `docm`, `pptm`, `ppsx`, `xlsm`, `xlsb` and four more are in `README.md:161-170` and are served. Rejected because a token in the card that no `[[rule]]` names fails `ow route lint` **check 10** from the other direction — [05](../05-ingest-and-routing.md):642-645 asserts that the union of `unit.format` literals across the shipped rules equals the computed domain — so widening the card without widening `decode.office-native` and core's forty-eight-token table breaks a different check. The three files move together or not at all, and adding nine tokens is a scope increase this ADR has no row for. **Pre-committed:** the extensions arrive as one change to all three files when a fixture class exists for them, and `V01-17` is what will notice they are missing.

### Ship a `derive.figure.vlm` pass so the charter's `omniweave-vision` row becomes true

The strongest form of this is not sentiment: `parse.page.olmocr` already puts torch and transformers on the machine for anyone who ingests a scan, the marginal install cost of a figure/chart pass is near zero, and the `caption` lane at `REPAIR` already flags picture blocks that nothing then reads ([05](../05-ingest-and-routing.md):3131). Rejected because a pass is not a driver id — it needs a `pass_id`, a rank and phase, a lane, `cost_class`, a `[capability.derive]` block, a distribution row, an `enable` entry, a Spend estimate and a slice in the quality gates, and `06` had every opportunity to write them and did not. Adding a `billed_api`-adjacent pass by ADR, unpriced and unmeasured, is precisely the scope explosion `R-A1` ([17](../17-risks.md):262) ranks at 4x4. Named out of tree, it can arrive on evidence.

### Amend charter §1 to strike "email, HTML" instead of relying on `V01-17`

Close, and it is one of the two closers `00#V-Q6` itself names. Its case: a reader who lands on charter §1's first sentence has no reason to look at `00` §2.1, and the erratum table exists so that a reader landing on a superseded line finds out. Rejected on scope, not on principle. Charter §1's sentence describes what omniweave is *for*; `00` §2.1's table describes what it *ships*, and the two differing is not an error but the shape of a v1 release — striking email and HTML from the purpose would misstate the project to save a lookup. The distinction is load-bearing enough that `V01-17` was written with an explicit second branch for it, and using an erratum here would set the precedent that every unshipped ambition needs one.

### Take `min on (-len(alias_set), -doc_count, key)` and delete `merge_hops`

The register's own alternative, and it is strong: three durable per-entity attributes, order-independent **by construction** rather than by a rule someone must remember, no dependence on the pair set at all, and the closest analogue of graphify's `_pick_winner` (`dedup.py:858-869`). It would delete the rebuild-from-log rule, the `06#N4` gate hole and an entire class of full-versus-incremental drift in one move. Rejected because the charter's rule is settled law that [06](../06-structure-extraction.md) already followed, and because `doc_count` is *not* more durable than it looks: it moves on every ingest, so a survivor elected under it flips when a document is added — trading drift-between-build-modes for drift-over-time, which is harder to gate because there is no second build to diff against. This was the second-closest call in the ADR and it is the reason D8.5 is an interim position with `06#N4` named as its trigger rather than a flat ratification.

## Open

**Nothing in this ADR's rows is left undecided.** Two adjacent questions are closed *by naming their precondition* rather than by answering them, which is recorded here so a reader does not mistake the anchor for an omission:

- **A `parse` Lane for time-based media** (`00#V-Q7`, v2.0) needs a temporal member on `Locus.precision` and a time branch on `OriginSpan` before it is a Driver question at all. What closes it: a `SCHEMA` major proposal under [charter](../_notes/charter.md) §6.6's two-release window. `R-T1` ([17](../17-risks.md):191) prices a structural model miss as the most expensive outcome in the plan and names "a time address for media" as one of its three examples, so this is a deliberate v2 anchor and not a gap.
- **The nine further anydoc extensions** (`docm`, `pptm`, `ppsx`, `ppsm`, `pps`, `pot`, `xlsm`, `xlsb`, and the `.doc` legacy alias's second media type) are out until a fixture class exists for them, per the rejected alternative above. What closes it: one change to `[capability] formats`, `decode.office-native` and core's forty-eight-token table together, with a gate slice. `V01-17` will not flag their absence — they are not format classes in §2.1 — so the trigger is a user report or `12#P-Q9`'s re-measurement, and that asymmetry is stated rather than hidden.
